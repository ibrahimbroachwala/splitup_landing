# Brief: Add Business pricing to the Splitup landing page

## Goal
Give tour operators, event planners and organizations a clear paid path, without changing the consumer pricing story. The page currently mentions business use only in a footnote ("Business pricing available on request").

## Principles
- Keep the existing look, tone and section order. Add to the page, don't redesign it.
- Business buys capacity and support, not locked features. Keep the line "Every feature is unlocked in every group, free or paid."
- Joining groups stays free for everyone, including participants of business customers.
- Do not change the "No subscriptions" promise. Business is sold as a prepaid 12-month group allowance, invoiced, with no auto-renewal.

## 1. Pricing section edits
- Unlimited card: change "Create as many as you like" to wording that states the fair-use limit up front, e.g. "Unlimited groups. Fair use: up to 30 active groups."
- Footnote: keep the "personal, non-commercial use" and Terms link, but replace "Business pricing available on request" with a pointer to the new Business section below.
- Do not change the existing pack options or prices. They follow the store listings.

## 2. New Business section (directly below the pricing block)
Heading: "Running trips or events for clients?"

Intro: Splitup Business is for tour operators, event planners and organizations that create groups for travelers or participants.

Points to include:
- Up to 250 groups per 12 months
- Price shown in both currencies: $549 / €500
- Participants join free, always
- Priority support and invoices
- Offline multi-currency included as soon as it ships (label it "coming soon")
- Need more? Add 50-group packs anytime ($109 / €100)

Button: "Get in touch" opens an email to the existing support address with a prefilled subject "Splitup Business enquiry". A short form is optional.

Short definition line under the points: "A group counts when it is created, whether it is later archived or deleted."

## 3. FAQ additions
- "Can I use Splitup for my business?" Yes. Personal plans are for non-commercial use. Organizations creating groups for clients should use Business.
- "What counts as a group on Business?" Each group created within your 12 months, including archived ones. Joining a group never counts.
- "Do my customers pay?" No. Anyone you invite joins free.
- "Can I get an invoice?" Yes, on request for Business.
- Update the existing "Is there a subscription?" answer to mention that Business is a prepaid annual allowance, invoiced, not a recurring charge.

## 4. Terms page
Add plain-language definitions of commercial use, the 30 active group limit on Unlimited, and the Business allowance (250 groups per 12 months, overage packs, no auto-renewal).

## 5. Do not include
- Customer names or usage numbers
- Per-group costs, margins or custom development pricing
- Any feature that has not shipped, other than the labeled "coming soon" item
- A competitor comparison table

## 6. Check before finishing
- Consistency across the hero, meta description, pricing intro and FAQ about subscriptions
- Layout on mobile, and in light and dark mode
- All new copy is plain text in the page, not in images
