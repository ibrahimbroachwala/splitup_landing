Privacy Policy for Splitup

Effective Date: 12th April, 2025

Splitup ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application, Splitup (the "App").

Information Collection and Usage

1. Authentication

  • We use Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In for authentication purposes only
  • We do not store or collect any additional personal information beyond what is provided through these authentication services
  • Basic profile information (name and email) from your Google or Apple account is used solely for account identification

2. App Data

  • Expense Information: Information you input about expenses, bills, and payments
  • Group Information: Names of expense groups you create

This information is stored securely and used only to provide the expense splitting functionality.

3. Device Information

  • Basic device information (device type and app version) for troubleshooting
  • No personal identifiers are collected from your device

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  1. Authenticate your account
  2. Provide expense splitting functionality
  3. Maintain and improve the App
  4. Respond to your support requests

Data Storage and Security

  • All expense data is stored securely on our servers
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information
  • We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Apple Sign-In

These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data by:

  1. Going to App Settings
  2. Selecting "Delete Account"
  3. Confirming the deletion

Upon deletion, all your expense data and account information will be permanently removed from our servers.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."
