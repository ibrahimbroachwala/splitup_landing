Splitup

Split. Remind. Settle.

Group expenses and bills with friends, fair and simple. Track shared costs, settle up in a tap, and keep everyone square — on iOS and Android.

Features

  • Add expenses in seconds Enter an amount, pick who paid, and split it equally or customize each share.
  • Manage groups Create groups for trips, roommates, or regular hangouts and track balances per group.
  • Settle up in a tap See who owes what to whom, and mark expenses settled the moment they're paid.
  • Works with your group's currency Split fair, every time — no matter where your group is spending.
  • Sign in securely Google and Apple Sign-In only — no extra passwords to remember.
